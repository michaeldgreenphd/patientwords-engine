   `cost_basis` is `cumulative_from_records`: `cost_usd` = $17.8875 is the whole six-model,
   1,764-call elicitation campaign, while `run_cost_usd` = **$6.0936** is what ran today

   first-seen sidecar's full cumulative amount to `run_utc`'s day, so ≈**$11.79** actually

and 2026-08-07 carries an owner-authorized $10 raise in `ops/budget_overrides.json`. But
the daily guard is now the study's least trustworthy number, and it is the one the brief

*Feed-forward:* `check_ceilings` should compare **per lane** and prefer `run_cost_usd` over
