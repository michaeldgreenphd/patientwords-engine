## 1 · What the study is

Two public repos, sibling checkouts:

| Repo | Path | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `michaeldgreenphd/patientwords-engine` | `/home/user/patientwords-engine` | measurement, analysis, exporters, ops |

Working branch on both: `claude/gemma-clinical-colloquial-interp-mavx04`.
Owner: Michael D. Green.

The question: when a person describes a medical situation in ordinary words
instead of clinical terminology, does a language model's computation change —
and does its *advice* change with it? Three measurement arms:

- **Next-token** — for a matched clinical/patient sentence pair, the probability
  the model assigns to the substantive medical continuation. The gap is the
  **language penalty**. 10 models measured.
- **Circuit** — hosted attribution graphs from Neuronpedia's circuit-tracer,
  feature-tagged. **Only `gemma-2-2b` produces hosted graphs.** Every other
  model degrades to `NullFetcher`, so its `clinical_mass ≈ 0.0` is an artifact,
  not a finding.
- **Advice** — deployed assistants asked the same situation phrased two ways,
  responses scored against a rubric by an LLM judge with human-coded agreement.

Nothing here produces medical advice to be acted on. It measures model behavior.

  an in-client approval the orchestrator session cannot satisfy. The doc
  describing it read as live until 2026-08-03.

**Recommendation for this session: keep the working session and the ops Routine
separate.** Bind the daily cycle to a fresh session per firing
(`create_new_session_on_fire: true`) so a reclaim of the interactive session
cannot stop measurement, and so the ops cycle re-orients from
`docs/routine_standing_prompt.md` + the repos each time rather than from a long
conversation's memory. **This requires the owner to approve the Routine
in-client** — it cannot be done from inside a session. Ask for it early.

If the owner prefers the current pace, that is a legitimate choice — but then the
briefs must say "runs when the session runs", not "daily", and multi-day gaps
stop being reported as anomalies.

## 9 · Session hygiene that paid off

- **Read the actual job log before diagnosing a CI failure.** Seven meditron runs
  were lost to a misdiagnosis that survived because nobody opened the log. The
  real cause: `commit_outputs` defaults to `false` on the push path, so the run
  measured everything and discarded it at the commit gate. `fire_trigger.py` now
  refuses a fire that omits the key.
- **The recorded diagnosis is often wrong.** The mitigation-cost gap was recorded
  as "the workflow glob drops sidecars". Nothing had ever written one.
- **When exact numbers are unrecoverable, say so and label the estimate.**
  Historical translation API usage was never recorded. The backfill is imputed
  from per-call text at ~4 chars/token, and every sidecar it writes carries
  `imputed: true` plus its method.
- **Run gates bare.** `python scripts/claim_check.py | tail` reports tail's exit
  status, and a failing gate looks green.
