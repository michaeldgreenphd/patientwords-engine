# PatientWords Study Analysis and Scenario Gap Report

> **Scope note:** This document summarizes the current experimental lanes, committed findings, and documented measurement limitations in `patientwords-engine` and `patientwords`, followed by exact counts of scenario coverage gaps in `patientwords/data/simulated_scenarios.json` (`n = 1,774` published scenarios across 27 batches) and `patientwords/data/urgency_shift.json`. All new candidate stimuli in `candidates.json` (`n = 84` minimal pairs) are research measurement stimuli, not medical advice or clinical findings.

## 1. Experiments Running Now and Their File Paths

### A. Next-Token Probability and Attribution Graph Measurement
1. **Hosted attribution-graph tracing (`gemma-2-2b`)**:
   - **Files**: `medlang_circuits/batch_eval.py`, `medlang_circuits/graph_client.py`, `medlang_circuits/pipeline.py`, `.github/workflows/circuit_trace_evaluation.yml` (trigger: `.github/trigger/circuit-trace.json`), exported via `scripts/export_frontend_simulated.py` to `patientwords/data/simulated_scenarios.json`.
   - **What it does**: Sends matched clinical and patient sentence prefixes to Neuronpedia (`gemmascope-transcoder-16k` on `gemma-2-2b`) to measure the next-token probability on `intended_target`, top-K token spreads, and sparse-autoencoder (SAE) attribution graphs showing which internal features drive or suppress the target continuation.
2. **Open-model CPU next-token logits evaluation**:
   - **Files**: `scripts/logits_eval.py`, `.github/workflows/logits_evaluation.yml` (trigger: `.github/trigger/logits-eval.json`), `scripts/paired_stats_rigor.py`, exported to `patientwords/data/model_stats.json`.

3. **Care-urgency tier shift analysis**:
   - **Files**: `scripts/urgency_shift.py`, `data/urgency_tiers.draft.json`, `scripts/embed_scenario_joins.py`, exported to `patientwords/data/urgency_shift.json`.
   - **What it does**: Maps the top-1 and top-K predicted next tokens under clinical and patient wording onto a 5-level care-urgency ladder (`4 = emergency care`, `3 = specialist care`, `2 = generalist professional care`, `1 = self-treatment / OTC`, `0 = no care action / comfort / social`, `null = excluded modifier/function word`) and classifies top-1 flips into `downgrade`, `upgrade`, `lateral`, or `uninformative`.
4. **Dialect and 4-quadrant orthogonal decomposition**:

   - **What it does**: Separates the effect of the symptom term from the surrounding carrier sentence by either holding the clinical term fixed across eight dialect/register frames (`dialects_20260708T215356Z`, 20 items × 8 variants = 160 cells) or crossing clinical/patient terms with clinical/patient carrier frames in a 2×2 design (`quadrants_*`).

## 2. What the Experiments Have Found So Far and Their Exact Caveats

### A. Next-Token Penalty and Care-Urgency Downgrades (`model_stats.json`, `urgency_shift.json`, `docs/findings_synthesis.md`)
- **Measured results**:
  - Across all 10 open models in `patientwords/data/model_stats.json` (observational `pairs_*` batches, phrase-deduped with seed 7 and 5,000 bootstrap replicates), patient wording reduces the probability assigned to `intended_target`, and no model's 95% confidence interval (or 10-model simultaneous Bonferroni 99.5% CI) crosses zero:
    - Confirmatory pre-registered models: `gemma-2-2b` (`n = 1,796` unique phrases, mean penalty `-0.0308`, BH $q = 8.73 \times 10^{-8}$), `gemma-3-4b-it` (`n = 1,326`, mean `-0.0509`, BH $q = 6.34 \times 10^{-7}$), `qwen3-4b` (`n = 1,120`, mean `-0.0457`, BH $q = 2.08 \times 10^{-8}$), and `qwen3-1.7b` (`n = 1,264`, mean `-0.0445`, BH $q = 1.86 \times 10^{-4}$).
    - Post-registration exploratory models: `medgemma-4b-it` (`n = 1,864`, mean `-0.0480`), `olmo-2-1b` (`n = 1,060`, mean `-0.0417`), `gemma-2-2b-it` (`n = 1,058`, mean `-0.0403`), `meditron3-8b` (`n = 1,864`, mean `-0.0365`), `llama-3.2-3b` (`n = 1,070`, mean `-0.0305`), and `apertus-8b-meditronfo` (`n = 1,864`, mean `-0.0293`).
  - When the top-1 predicted token changes between clinical and patient wording and both tokens map to `data/urgency_tiers.draft.json`, downgrades outnumber upgrades on every model (`urgency_shift.json` `per_model_deduped`: e.g., `gemma-2-2b` 106 downgrades vs. 41 upgrades; `medgemma-4b-it` 97 vs. 29; `meditron3-8b` 86 vs. 29; `gemma-3-4b-it` 80 vs. 26; `qwen3-1.7b` 76 vs. 36; `qwen3-4b` 74 vs. 18; `apertus-8b-meditronfo` 74 vs. 29; `olmo-2-1b` 68 vs. 15; `llama-3.2-3b` 59 vs. 23; `gemma-2-2b-it` 55 vs. 25).
- **Exact documented caveats (`AGENTS.md`, `docs/agent_reference.md`, `docs/findings_synthesis.md`)**:
  1. **Hosted target-read prefix bug before 2026-10-07**: Until `2026-10-07`, the hosted Neuronpedia path used a 4-character case-blind bidirectional prefix match (`scripts/audit_target_reads.py`, `data/audits/target_reads_20261007.json`): 63 hosted results carried another token's probability (40 in a published field, e.g., `" ant"` read as `" anti"` on index 20 of `pairs_20260710T011743Z`), and 23 measured a leading piece of a whole-vocabulary word (16 published). Of 262 published substituted rows, 251 used the unscreened `top_logit_fallback`, 8 came from the prefix match itself, and 3 from the logits lane's bare-space first token.
  2. **Negative-control non-specificity (`docs/negative_control_20260904.md`)**: Non-medical register swaps (formal vs. everyday non-clinical vocabulary) also shift next-token probabilities, so the probability penalty is not specific to clinical vocabulary alone.
  3. **Draft urgency vocabulary without clinician sign-off**: `data/urgency_tiers.draft.json` is explicitly labeled `owner-reviewed v1 · domain review pending`; 419 of its 659 listed tokens map to `null` (excluded), and unlisted low-frequency tokens default to excluded.
  4. **Paraphrase noise and condition-equivalence drift**: Innocuous meaning-preserving paraphrases move a single pair's probability by `~0.064` on average (`docs/findings_synthesis.md` §7), so no single pair is evidence. In the blind 20-pair owner review (`docs/stimulus_qc_v1.json`, `docs/equivalence_screen_design.md`), 3 of 20 pairs (all 3 in the flip half) failed condition equivalence on the patient side.
  5. **Low pairwise downgrade overlap across models**: In `urgency_shift.json`, pairwise Jaccard overlap of downgraded phrases across model pairs ranges from `0.103` (`meditron3-8b` & `qwen3-1.7b`) to `0.280` (`gemma-2-2b` & `qwen3-4b`).
  6. **bfloat16 resolution on CPU logits**: `scripts/logits_eval.py` runs in `bfloat16` (`~0.008` 1-ULP grid spacing near $p = 1$).

### B. Attribution Circuits, Lens Readouts, and Steering (`jlens_insights.json`, `docs/feature_experiments_design.md`)
- **Measured results**:
  - Traced clinical and patient circuits on `gemma-2-2b` share a median of `46.7%` of their active features (`n = 365` pairs); clinical and off-target features sit at nearly identical mean depth (`~20.0` vs. `~20.6` of 26 layers).
  - On the 5-pair steerable subset (`trace_out/boostgrid_*`), boosting a pair's own top-5 clinical features at strength 5 recovered `5/5` targets, boosting ranks 6–10 recovered `3/4` (replicated on `boostgrid_lowrank_r2`), and a 5-random-feature placebo recovered `0/5`.
  - In `patientwords/data/jlens_insights.json` (`n = 2,133` pairs after excluding 190 holdout rows), clinical formation median layer is `18` (IQR `17–21`, `n = 1,543`) and patient formation median is `19` (IQR `17–22`, `n = 1,378`), with `171` `capture`, `126` `hijack`, `1,369` `held`, and `467` `unreadable` at top-8 persistence window $k = 2$.
- **Exact documented caveats**:
  1. **Host-side lens drift and overturned window-sensitivity claim**: Neuronpedia's hosted Jacobian lens changed twice on the host side (`2026-07-31` and `2026-08-14`). After the `2026-10-31` re-measurement of July readouts, the earlier census claim that `capture` exceeds `hijack` at every window $k$ no longer holds (`k = 2` shifted to `206` capture vs. `223` hijack across re-measured readouts, even though the older `2026-08-21` snapshot in `patientwords/data/jlens_insights.json` still records `207` vs. `206` with `window_capture_exceeds_hijack_at_every_k: true`).
  2. **Exploratory status of lens readouts and steering**: `jlens_insights.json` is explicitly labeled `EXPLORATORY - formation-depth analytics from landed lens readouts; no pre-registered endpoint`. Cross-pair feature transfer (Design A) and residual-vs-SAE comparison (Design B) in `docs/feature_experiments_design.md` and lens steering in `docs/lens_steering_design.md` are small-$n$ or unexecuted designs. The 1,038 dialect/quadrant lens pairs landed on `2026-11-01` (`trace_out/*__lenspairs__jlens_gemma-2-2b/`) are excluded from the observational census and remain unanalyzed.

### C. Single-Turn Deployed Advice and Multi-Turn Petri Audits (`advice_scenarios.json`, `docs/petri_wave3_design.md`, `docs/exploratory/xjudge_20261031.md`)
- **Measured results**:
  - **Single-turn advice (`patientwords/data/advice_scenarios.json`, `n = 99` scenarios)**: Unlike next-token completion on small base models, deployed chat assistants show near-zero net urgency tier shift between clinical and patient wording across 6 of 7 models in single-turn Q&A. However, patient wording reduces the rate at which responses explicitly name a medical specialty (`scripts/referral_destination.py`).
  - **Multi-turn Petri Wave 2 (`data/petri/w2_register_contrast.json`, `docs/petri_wave3_design.md`)**: Across 8 scenarios and 35 multi-turn triples on `claude-haiku-4-5` (judged by `claude-haiku-4-5`), colloquial patient register received lower mean urgency than clinical register in `27` of `35` triples (`6` positive, `2` tied, two-sided sign test $p = 0.00032$). Exchange 1 alone was not significant (`9` negative, `3` positive, `23` tied), whereas Exchanges 6–10 showed divergence (`19` vs. `3` with `8` tied). In the 3-arm style-vs-vocabulary split (`n = 15` triples), style (`colloquial` vs. `lay_careful`) was `13` vs. `2` ($p = 0.0074$, Holm $p = 0.022$) while vocabulary (`lay_careful` vs. `clinical`) was `7` vs. `5` with `3` tied ($p = 0.774$), and their paired difference (`10` vs. `3`, $p = 0.092$, Holm $p = 0.185$) carried the registered statement `not_separated`.
  - **Exploratory Cross-Judge & Equity v2 Pilot (`docs/exploratory/xjudge_20261031.md`, campaign `xjudge-v1`)**: Across 88 conversations (64 register + 24 equity v2) on `claude-haiku-4-5` and `gpt-5.4-mini` graded by three judges (`claude-haiku-4-5`, `gpt-5.4-mini`, `claude-haiku-5-5`), `claude-haiku-4-5` scored replies lower in urgency than `gpt-5.4-mini` and `claude-haiku-5-5`, with a larger gap on GPT-5.4 mini replies (`+0.35`) than on Claude replies (`+0.15`, difference `0.20 [0.08, 0.30]`) concentrated in low-acuity turns 1–7. On the 24 equity v2 conversations (sickle cell acute chest and diabetic foot sepsis with housing/stigma barriers vs. work-convenience controls), all `6/6` barrier conversations held at emergency care (`4.00`) at turn 9 after the turn-8 red flag, though neither model mentioned EMTALA rights for the uninsured patient.
- **Exact documented caveats**:
  1. **Specialty naming is not recommendation**: `data/referral_destination_vocab.draft.json` (`draft pending owner + domain review`) counts substring mentions only; a negated mention (`"you do not need a cardiologist"`) is counted the same as a positive referral.
  2. **Withheld truncated Gemini advice runs**: In `advice_scenarios.json`, `1,064` records across `gemini-3.5-flash` and `gemini-3.1-pro-preview` are withheld due to provider reasoning-token truncation (`stop_reason=length`).
  3. **Exploratory status of `xjudge-v1` and unexecuted status of Wave 3 / Equity v3**: `docs/exploratory/xjudge_20261031.md` is explicitly a **1-epoch exploratory report with no clinician review**; it must not be cited as a confirmed finding. Petri Wave 3 (`docs/petri_wave3_design.md`, `docs/framework/petri_seeds_w3.draft.json`), Equity v3 (`docs/framework/petri_seeds_equity_v3.draft.json`), and `barrier_help.draft.json` are **draft designs awaiting physician review** (gate extended to `2027-01-31`) and have not been executed.
  4. **Action-label pilot, PAB, and Causalab/J++ limits**: The assisted action-label pilot (`docs/exploratory/action_pilot.md`) stopped on parser/offset/fence errors in all three bounded attempts (`40320384841`, `40344924588`, `40510625603`), and `scripts/action_structured_annotations.py` has `execution_enabled: false`. PAB (`docs/pab_integration_layers.md`) carries CC-BY-NC-4.0 non-commercial restrictions and draft unreviewed clinical term lists. Causalab/J++ (`docs/exploratory/methods_integration.md`, `docs/exploratory/baseline_ci.md`) has unknown historical checkpoint model/tokenizer revisions and no executed real-model Qwen baseline run.

## 3. Counted Coverage Gaps in `patientwords/data/simulated_scenarios.json`

All counts below are computed directly over the `1,774` published scenarios (`1,772` unique `(clinical_prompt, patient_prompt)` pairs across `27` batches) in `patientwords/data/simulated_scenarios.json` and the `659`-token vocabulary in `data/urgency_tiers.draft.json`.

### Gap 1: Emergency Care (Tier 4) Targets Are Only 1.13% of Published Scenarios (`20 / 1,774`)
When each scenario's `intended_target` is mapped against `data/urgency_tiers.draft.json`:
- **Tier 4 (`emergency care`)**: **`20` scenarios (`1.13%`)** (`hospital`: 18, `emergency`: 2, `ER`: 0 as `intended_target`, `ed`: 0, `ers`: 0). Furthermore, `17` of those `20` Tier 4 scenarios were added in the final two batches (`pairs_20260809T172338Z` with 3 and `pairs_20260811T190638Z` with 14); across the first 25 batches (`1,647` scenarios), only **`3` scenarios (`0.18%`)** had a Tier 4 `intended_target` (indices `118`, `786`, and `1038`).
- **Tier 3 (`specialist care`)**: **`94` scenarios (`5.30%`)** (plus unmapped specialist titles such as `neurologist`: 69, `gastroenterologist`: 28, `urologist`: 27, `cardiologist`: 22, `gynecologist`: 18, `rheumatologist`: 12).
- **Tier 2 (`generalist professional care`)**: **`254` scenarios (`14.32%`)**.
- **Tier 1 (`self-treatment / OTC`)**: **`337` scenarios (`19.00%`)**.
- **Tier 0 (`no care action / comfort / social`)**: **`69` scenarios (`3.89%`)**.
- **Tier `null` (`excluded modifier / function word`)**: **`128` scenarios (`7.22%`)**.
- **Unlisted in `urgency_tiers.draft.json`**: **`872` scenarios (`49.15%`)** (nearly half of all published `intended_target` tokens—such as diagnosis nouns `infection`, `condition`, `disorder`, `thyroid`, `menopause`, or multi-subword targets—are not in the urgency-tier vocabulary, yielding `1,006 / 1,774` scenarios with `urgency.flip_class = null` and `561 / 1,774` with `flip_class = "uninformative"` on `gemma-2-2b`).

### Gap 3: Wording Types Underrepresented or Absent in `simulated_scenarios.json`
- **Literacy and natural patient misspellings**: **`0` scenarios** in `simulated_scenarios.json` (`data/measured/imported_pairs.json` has `1` `charlie/charley horse` pair and `1` `ihal` target typo, but the `1,774`-pair main corpus has zero phonetic or typographical symptom misspellings).

The separate 20-item `patientwords/data/dialects.json` matrix (`dialects_20260708T215356Z`) holds the **clinical term** fixed (`clinically depressed`, `bronchitis`, `syncopal episodes`, etc.) and varies only the carrier sentence; no published minimal pair tests a dialect or L2 symptom description inside a shared carrier frame.
- **Minimizing and hedged symptom framing**: **`14` scenarios (`0.79%`)** contain mild hedges (`kind of`, `a little`), and **`0` scenarios** test explicit self-dismissal prefixes (`"probably nothing, just..."`, `"probably just pulled a muscle, though..."`) on high-acuity red-flag or specialist conditions.

### Gap 4: High-Risk Patient Groups and Medication Safety Contexts
- **Older age (`65+` / geriatric physiology)**: **`0` first-person scenarios** in `simulated_scenarios.json` state an older age (`4` third-person scenarios mention `"Grandpa"`, `0` state an age or geriatric red flag such as acute delirium or giant cell arteritis).
